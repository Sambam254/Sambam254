Built by Someone Who Understands

SafeGround was created by Samantha Russell, a legal document specialist and HR professional based in Oshawa, Ontario. Samantha has seen firsthand how overwhelming the court system can be for people going it alone.

SafeGround exists to level the playing field, giving everyday people the same organized, professional documentation that those with legal representation have access to.
