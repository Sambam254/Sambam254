Why It Works

The Problem With Most Resumes

Most resumes never reach a human.

Over 75% of resumes are filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a hiring manager ever sees them. ATS software scans for specific keywords, formatting, and structure. If your resume doesn't pass the scan, it doesn't matter how qualified you are.

At One Stop Therapy Shop, every resume is built to clear ATS filters first, then impress the human reading it second. That's the right order.

What makes our resumes different:

  • →Written by an HR professional with real recruitment experience
  • →ATS-tested keyword placement for your specific industry
  • →Formatted for Canadian employers and hiring standards
  • →Accomplishment-focused language (not just job descriptions)
  • →Delivered in under 4 hours — not days or weeks
