About

Meet Samantha

I'm Samantha Russell — in 2015 I graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in Criminology & Justice. I went on to take further studies in HR, and in 2017 I opened my first small business. I'm based in Oshawa, Ontario, and I built One Stop Therapy Shop because I've seen first-hand how overwhelming the legal system and job market can be, especially when you're navigating them alone.

With a background in human resources and recruitment, I understand what employers want — and I know how to translate your experience into results.

On the legal side, I bring structure, clarity, and professionalism to help self-represented litigants and legal practitioners alike.

My mission is simple: give you the tools, documents, and support you need to move forward with confidence.
