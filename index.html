I'm Samantha Russell — an HR professional and recruiter based in Oshawa, Ontario, and I built this practice because I've spent nearly a decade watching talented people get passed over by broken hiring processes.

My background includes a Bachelor of Arts in Criminology and Justice, HR studies at Durham College, and hands-on talent acquisition experience including recruitment at CAMH — one of Canada's most respected mental health organizations. I am currently working in Talent Acquisition while building toward an HR Generalist and, eventually, HR Manager role. That career progression isn't just a goal — it's the lens through which I approach every client engagement. I understand the hiring process from both sides of the table.

In 2017, I opened my first business. What started as a solo services practice grew into One Stop Therapy Shop — a brand built on the belief that professional-quality support shouldn't be reserved for people who can afford premium retainers. That mission hasn't changed. The focus has.

Today, every service I offer is designed to answer one question: "Why should I trust Samantha Russell with my career?" I hope what you find here answers it.
