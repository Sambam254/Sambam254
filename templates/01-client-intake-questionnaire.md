## CareerBoost — Style Guide (Internal)

### Voice & Tone
- **Professional but not stiff** — use active voice, confident language
- **No fluff** — every bullet must justify its space
- **Quantify everything** — "$", "%", numbers, time saved, revenue generated
- **No first-person** ("I", "my") — use implied first-person

### Formatting Rules
- **File naming convention:** `{client-name}_Resume_v1.pdf`, `{client-name}_CoverLetter_v1.pdf`
- **Font:** ATS-safe — Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, or Georgia (body 10-11pt)
- **Margins:** 0.5"-0.75" all around (max text per page without looking cramped)
- **Length:** 1 page for <10 yrs exp; 2 pages max for 10+
- **File delivery:** PDF (for client) + DOCX (for them to edit)

### ATS Optimization Rules
- Use standard section headers: **Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications**
- No columns, tables, graphics, or text boxes (ATS can't parse them)
- No headers/footers with critical info
- Spell out abbreviations on first use
- Include a "Skills" section with both hard and soft skills
- Use keywords from the job description naturally in context
- File format: .docx preferred for ATS; .pdf as secondary
