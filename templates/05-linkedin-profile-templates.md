## Headline, About, and Experience sections

## 1. HEADLINE TEMPLATES

Your LinkedIn headline is the most SEO-critical part of your profile. It's searchable and is the first thing recruiters see.

### Formula
**[Job Title] | [Specialty 1] & [Specialty 2] | Helping [target audience] [achieve outcome] | [Key Metric]**

### Templates

**Template A — The Specialist (Tech/Engineering)**
> "Senior [Role] | [Stack/Tech] Expert | Building [type of systems] at scale | [Metric]"

*Example:*
> Senior Data Engineer | Python, Spark & AWS | Building real-time data pipelines processing 50TB+ daily | 3x cost reduction

**Template B — The Results Driver (Sales/Marketing/PM)**
> "[Role] | Driving [Key Outcome] through [Method/Strategy] | [Industry] Leader | $[X]M in [Revenue/Savings]"

*Example:*
> Product Marketing Manager | Driving GTM strategy for B2B SaaS | 15+ product launches | $12M pipeline generated

**Template C — The Modern Hybrid (Design/Product/Creative)**
> "[Role] blending [Skill A] & [Skill B] | [Company/Industry] | [Notable project or brand]"

*Example:*
> Product Designer blending UX research & interaction design | Figma & Design Systems | Led redesign for 2M+ user app

**Template D — Career Changer/Grad**
> "Aspiring [Target Role] | [Transferable Skill 1] & [Skill 2] background | [Education/Cert] | [Passion statement]"

*Example:*
> Aspiring Product Manager | Engineering & Data Analytics background | AWS Certified | Building products users love

### Headline Rules
- **220 character max** — make every character count
- Include 2-3 keywords from target job titles
- Include a number or metric if possible (years, $, %, users)
- No buzzwords: "results-oriented", "highly motivated", "team player"
- Tag your current company if it carries weight

## 2. ABOUT SECTION TEMPLATES

### Template A — The Narrative (3 paragraph structure)

> **Opening Hook (2-3 sentences)**
> I'm a [Role] with [X] years of experience helping [target audience] achieve [outcome]. I specialize in [Skill A], [Skill B], and [Skill C] — turning [problem/challenge] into [result].
>
> **Proof/Accomplishments (3-5 bullet points)**
> Throughout my career, I've:
> • [Quantified achievement 1 — with metric]
> • [Quantified achievement 2 — with metric]
> • [Quantified achievement 3 — with metric]
> • [Quantified achievement 4 — with metric]
>
> **What's Next + Call to Action**
> I'm currently exploring [type of role/industry] opportunities where I can [desired outcome]. If you're working on [relevant topic] or looking for a [role type], let's connect. DM me or email [email].

### Template B — The Punchy (short, high-impact, ideal if <5 years exp)

> **[Role]** turning **[complex thing]** into **[simple outcome]**.
>
> Currently at **[Company]** where I **[key responsibility]**.
> Previously at **[Past Co]** doing **[past responsibility]**.
>
> **Core toolkit:** [Skill 1] · [Skill 2] · [Skill 3] · [Tool 1] · [Tool 2]
>
> Open to **[role type]** roles. Let's talk: [email/LinkedIn DM]

### About Section Rules
- **2,600 character max** — aim for 1,000-2,000 characters
- Use paragraph breaks liberally (wall of text = ignored)
- Include 5-10 keywords from target roles
- End with a call to action (recruiters need a next step)
- No clichés: "passionate", "dedicated", "proven track record"

## 3. EXPERIENCE SECTION TEMPLATES

### Per-Role Format

> **[Job Title]**
> [Company Name] · [Employment Type (Full-time/Contract)]
> [Month Year] - [Month Year] · [Duration] · [Location]
>
> • [Bullet 1 — same resume-quality, quantified bullet]
> • [Bullet 2 — same resume-quality, quantified bullet]
> • [Bullet 3 — same resume-quality, quantified bullet]
>
> **Skills:** [Skill 1] · [Skill 2] · [Skill 3] · [Tool 1] · [Tool 2]
> *(Use LinkedIn's skill tagging feature — each skill tag links to your Skills section)*

### Optimization Tips for Each Role

1. **Write 2-3 bullets minimum** — LinkedIn allows up to 2,000 characters per role
2. **Don't copy-paste your resume verbatim** — use slightly different phrasing for broader keyword coverage
3. **Add media when possible** — attach presentations, GitHub repos, articles, or projects to each role
4. **Tag relevant skills** in the "Skills" line — each tag signals LinkedIn's algorithm
5. **Use the "Pinned" section** for your best media/featured content

## 4. ADDITIONAL LINKEDIN OPTIMIZATIONS

### Profile Photo
- Professional headshot (or high-quality recent photo)
- Neutral background or your actual workspace
- No selfies, group photos, or vacation shots
- Profile photos with smiles get 30% more connection requests

### Featured Section (Prime Real Estate)
Pin these 3 things:
1. **Your optimized resume** (as a PDF attachment)
2. **A portfolio piece** or case study
3. **A recent post** or article you wrote (or a recommendation)

### Open To Work Settings
- Set to "Recruiters Only" (not public) unless you want everyone to know
- Add target roles (up to 5 titles)
- Add target locations
- Update weekly

### Skills & Endorsements
- List 20-50 relevant skills
- Reorder so the most important for your target role appear first
- Ask 3-5 colleagues to endorse your top 3 skills

### Recommendations
- Request 1-2 recommendations per quarter
- From: managers, peers, direct reports, or clients
- Give before you ask (write them a great recommendation first)

## 5. LINKEDIN KEYWORD OPTIMIZATION GUIDE

### Where to Place Keywords (ranked by importance)
1. **Headline** (highest search weight)
2. **About section** first 2 lines
3. **Current job title** and company
4. **Skills section**
5. **Experience bullet points**
6. **Education field of study**

### How to Find the Right Keywords
1. Search your target role on LinkedIn and look at the top 10 profiles
2. Note recurring terms in their headlines and About sections
3. Search for your target job title on Indeed/Glassdoor
4. Pick 10-15 most common terms across 3-5 job descriptions
5. Distribute them naturally across your profile

### Tools for Keyword Research
- LinkedIn search autocomplete (type target role and see suggestions)
- Jobscan.co (compare your profile against job descriptions)
- SEMrush / Ahrefs (for SEO keyword data, if available)
