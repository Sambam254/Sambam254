# CareerBoost — Service Delivery Playbook
## Turn around a complete resume + LinkedIn overhaul in under 4 hours

---

## ⏱ Time Budget (4 Hours Total)

| Phase | Time | Activity |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Phase 1: Intake & Research** | 30 min | Collect client info, extract target keywords, analyze 3 target JDs |
| **Phase 2: Resume Writing** | 90 min | Write/rewrite resume using template; optimize every bullet |
| **Phase 3: Cover Letter** | 30 min | Write personalized cover letter for primary target role |
| **Phase 4: LinkedIn Optimization** | 45 min | Rewrite headline, about, and experience sections |
| **Phase 5: Quality Review** | 30 min | ATS scan, grammar check, consistency check, format export |
| **Phase 6: Delivery** | 15 min | Export PDF + DOCX, send to client, log deliverable |

---

## Phase 1: Intake & Research (30 min)

### Step 1.1 — Get Client Info
Send the client the intake questionnaire (`01-client-intake-questionnaire.md`). If the client doesn't fill it out, do a quick discovery call (15 min max) or extract from their current resume/LinkedIn.

### Step 1.2 — Keyword Extraction
1. Find 3 job descriptions for the client's target role (LinkedIn, Indeed, company career pages)
2. Extract the top 15-20 recurring keywords (tools, skills, methodologies, certifications)
3. Create a keyword list for reference during writing

### Step 1.3 — Identify "Story"
Determine the narrative arc:
- What's the client's career trajectory? (promotions, increasing scope, industry moves)
- What's their single most impressive achievement?
- What gap are they trying to fill / role are they targeting?

---

## Phase 2: Resume Writing (90 min)

### Step 2.1 — Choose Template
- **Classic ATS** → Corporate, finance, healthcare, traditional industries
- **Modern Creative** → Tech, startups, marketing, design, media

### Step 2.2 — Write Summary (10 min)
- 3-4 sentences
- Template: "[Years] of experience [core activity] for [target audience]. Expert in [Skill 1], [Skill 2], and [Skill 3]. [Notable career metric]. [What they bring next]."
- Include 2-3 target keywords naturally

### Step 2.3 — Rewrite Experience Bullets (60 min)
- Every bullet must follow: **Action Verb + What + How + Metric**
- Quantify everything — $, %, time saved, users, revenue
- Front-load keywords from the job descriptions
- Eliminate: "Responsible for", "Helped with", "Involved in", "Duties included"
- Strong action verb bank (see reference below)

### Step 2.4 — Build Skills & Competencies (10 min)
- Pull 15-20 relevant skills (mix of hard and soft)
- Group by category if using Classic ATS template
- Include target role keywords

### Step 2.5 — Education & Certifications (10 min)
- List degree, school, year
- Add relevant coursework, honors, or capstones
- Certifications that are relevant to target role

---

## Phase 3: Cover Letter (30 min)

### Step 3.1 — Research Target Company (5 min)
- Visit company website, recent blog posts, news
- Find 1 specific thing to reference (product launch, funding, mission)

### Step 3.2 — Write Cover Letter (25 min)
- Use `04-cover-letter-template.md`
- Customize the "hook" paragraph with research
- Connect each bullet to a specific requirement in the job description
- Keep to 1 page

---

## Phase 4: LinkedIn Optimization (45 min)

### Step 4.1 — Headline (5 min)
- Use `05-linkedin-profile-templates.md` for templates
- Must include: target role title + 2-3 keywords + 1 metric
- Max 220 characters — count them

### Step 4.2 — About Section (20 min)
- Use the Narrative template (3 paragraph structure)
- Adapt resume summary into opening hook
- Convert 3-5 resume bullets into bullet points
- End with call to action

### Step 4.3 — Experience Section (20 min)
- Convert resume bullets into LinkedIn-friendly version (slightly different phrasing)
- Add skill tags for each role
- Note: write "Present" not "Current" for dates

---

## Phase 5: Quality Review (30 min)

### Checklist

| Check | Pass? |
|-------|-------|
| No spelling or grammar errors | ☐ |
| Consistent voice (active, confident, no first-person) | ☐ |
| Every bullet has a number ($, %, time, users) | ☐ |
| No "responsible for / duties included" phrases | ☐ |
| Target keywords present in summary + experience | ☐ |
| Resume fits 1 page (or 2 if 10+ yrs) | ☐ |
| Margins 0.5"-0.75" | ☐ |
| Consistent date formatting (Month YYYY) | ☐ |
| Cover letter ≤ 1 page | ☐ |
| LinkedIn headline ≤ 220 chars | ☐ |
| LinkedIn About has call to action | ☐ |
| All links are correct and working | ☐ |

### Export Checklist
- [ ] Export resume as PDF (for client)
- [ ] Export resume as DOCX (for client to edit)
- [ ] Export cover letter as PDF
- [ ] Save files following naming convention: `{ClientName}_{Deliverable}_v1.pdf`

---

## Phase 6: Delivery (15 min)

1. Send deliverables to client with brief summary of changes
2. Ask for feedback / 5-star review
3. Log completed files in `/home/team/shared/deliveries/{client-name}/`
4. Note in leads database that deliverable is complete

## 🚫 Words to Eliminate

| Remove | Replace With |
|--------|-------------|
| Responsible for | Led, Drove, Owned |
| Helped with | Contributed to, Supported (or remove entirely) |
| Worked on | Built, Developed, Delivered |
| Involved in | (Remove — be specific) |
| Duties included | (Remove — use bullet format) |
| Team player | Demonstrated (in context) |
| Hardworking | (Remove — implied by results) |
| Results-oriented | (Remove — show the results) |
| Passionate about | (Remove — show it through outcomes) |
