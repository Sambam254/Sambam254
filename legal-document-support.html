Services

What Legal Document Support Includes

Professional document preparation and case organization support for every stage of your Ontario family court matter.

📋

Court Document Preparation

Applications, Answers, Affidavits, Financial Statements, and Notices — prepared and formatted to Ontario Court of Justice standards so your documents look as professional as those filed by lawyers.

📝

Affidavit Drafting

Clear, organized affidavit drafting that tells your story factually, chronologically, and in the format Ontario courts expect. Your affidavit is often the most important document in your case.

📁

Case Organization

Evidence, correspondence, and timeline organized into a clear, logical package that judges can follow easily. Organization is one of the most powerful advantages a self-represented litigant can have.

📬

Filing Guidance

Step-by-step courthouse filing instructions, service requirements, deadlines, and procedural guidance so you know exactly what to do, when, and how — specific to Ontario courts.

✉️

Correspondence Drafting

Professional letters to opposing parties, opposing counsel, and court offices — drafted clearly and appropriately to protect your position without escalating conflict.

🧭

Procedure Navigation

Understand motions, case conferences, settlement conferences, and trial prep — step by step, in plain language. Know what to expect before it happens.

Transparent Pricing

Sliding-Scale Fees. Professional Quality.

Legal fees in Ontario can run $300 to $500 per hour. Legal document support services are a fraction of that cost — and the fees slide based on your situation, not a flat rate designed for people with legal budgets.

You will always know the cost before any work begins. No retainers, no surprise billing, no hourly clock.
