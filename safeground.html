About SafeGround

What Is SafeGround?

SafeGround is a specialized court document preparation and case organization service created by Samantha Russell — legal document specialist, BA Criminology and Justice, and founder of One Stop Therapy Shop in Oshawa, Ontario.

SafeGround was built for one reason: self-represented litigants deserve professional, organized, court-ready documentation — the same quality that people with lawyers have access to — at a price that is actually affordable.

This is not generic legal aid. This is personalized, hands-on support from someone who understands Ontario family court procedures and the people navigating them alone.

Why SafeGround Is Different

  • ✓Sliding-scale fees — you pay what you can afford, not what lawyers charge
  • ✓Ontario-specific — built around Ontario Courts of Justice procedures
  • ✓Oshawa and Durham Region based — local knowledge, local context
  • ✓Document preparation, not legal advice — we help you present your situation professionally
  • ✓Ongoing support as your case develops, not a one-time transaction

What SafeGround Helps With

Support Across Every Stage of Your Case

📋

Court Document Preparation

Applications, Answers, Affidavits, Financial Statements — formatted to Ontario court standards so your documents look as professional as those filed by lawyers.

📁

Case Organization

Evidence, timeline, and arguments organized into a clear, logical package that judges can follow easily. Organization is one of the most powerful tools in a self-represented case.

📬

Filing Guidance

Step-by-step courthouse filing instructions, deadlines, and procedural support so you know exactly what to do, when, and how.

✉️

Correspondence Drafting

Professional letters to opposing parties, lawyers, and court offices — drafted clearly and appropriately to protect your position.

🧭

Procedure Navigation

Understand motions, case conferences, settlement conferences, and trial prep — step by step, in plain language.

💬

Ongoing Support

Email and document support as new developments arise throughout your case — because family court is rarely a single event.

Sliding-Scale Fees

Professional Support. Accessible Pricing.

SafeGround operates on a sliding-scale fee model. This means the cost is based on your situation and what you can reasonably afford — not a flat rate designed for people with legal budgets.

You will always know the cost before you commit. There are no surprise fees, retainers, or hourly billing. Just clear, fair pricing for the support you actually need.

Much Less Than a Lawyer

Legal fees in Ontario can run $300–$500 per hour. SafeGround provides professional document support at a fraction of that cost.

Sliding Scale

Your fee is based on what you can afford. We believe everyone deserves professional representation quality — regardless of income.

No Surprises

You will know exactly what you are paying before any work begins. Clear agreements, no hidden costs.
