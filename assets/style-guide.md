# Crown Signature — Visual Style Guide

## Brand Overview
**One Stop Therapy Shop** bridges professional excellence and holistic well-being.  
**Crown Signature** is the premium branding line that represents authority, elegance, and trust.

---

## 1. Logo
- **Primary Logo:** Crown emblem with "CROWN SIGNATURE" serif wordmark
- **File:** `/assets/crown-signature-logo.png`
- **Usage:** Always use the full-color version on light backgrounds. Monochrome version for dark backgrounds.
- **Clear Space:** Maintain padding equal to the height of the crown icon on all sides.
- **Never:** Stretch, rotate, add effects, or place on cluttered backgrounds.

## 2. Color Palette

| Color Name     | Hex       | Usage                                      |
|---------------|-----------|--------------------------------------------|
| Deep Green    | `#0A2E26` | Primary brand color, headers, nav, footer  |
| Warm Gold     | `#D4AF37` | Accent, CTAs, highlights, decorative lines |
| Ivory         | `#FAF7F2` | Backgrounds, cards, light sections         |
| Stone         | `#78716C` | Body text, secondary text                  |
| Pure White    | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on dark sections, text on dark       |
| Charcoal      | `#292524` | Footer text, strong contrast               |

### Gradients
- **Gold-to-Green Fade:** For premium decorative elements
- **Overlay:** Deep green at 20% opacity over hero images

## 3. Typography

### Headings
- **Font:** Playfair Display (serif)
- **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 700 (Bold)
- **Sizes:** H1: 4xl-6xl, H2: 3xl-4xl, H3: 2xl
- **Style:** Elegant, editorial, timeless
- **Usage:** All major headings, section titles, hero text

### Body Text
- **Font:** Inter (sans-serif)
- **Weights:** 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (Semibold)
- **Sizes:** Base: 16px, Lead: 18-20px, Small: 14px
- **Style:** Clean, modern, highly readable
- **Usage:** Paragraphs, navigation, captions, forms

## 4. Imagery Style

### Photography
- **Style:** Warm, natural light, soft focus
- **Subjects:** Professional settings, wellness/meditation, nature elements
- **Color Treatment:** Warm tone with slight desaturation, deep green and gold accents
- **Avoid:** Harsh flash, overly clinical/sterile imagery

### Brand Graphics
- Crown icon as signature mark
- Gold divider lines (20px wide, 1px height)
- Subtle geometric patterns (herringbone, chevron)
- Gold foil texture effects on premium elements

## 5. UI Elements

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Green background, White text, Rounded (rounded-md or rounded-full)
- **Secondary:** Gold background, White text
- **Ghost:** Border with transparent background, hover fills with brand color
- **Hover:** All buttons lighten by 10% opacity or shift to gold-on-green

### Cards
- **Background:** Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) or White
- **Border:** 1px solid Stone-100, or Gold border for featured items
- **Shadow:** Subtle, elevates on hover (shadow-xl transition)
- **Border Radius:** rounded-xl (12px)

### Navigation
- Sticky top bar, white background
- Text links in Stone-600, hover to Deep Green
- CTA button in Deep Green with rounded-full shape
- Mobile: hamburger menu with slide-down panel

## 6. Voice & Tone

- **Professional** but **warm** and **approachable**
- Use "we" and "you" to create connection
- Emphasize exclusivity and premium quality
- Language: confident, holistic, empowering
- Tagline: *"Holistic Excellence in Wellness & Professional Identity."*

## 7. Application Examples

### Crown Signature Products Page
- Product cards in 3-column grid

- "Purchase Now" buttons linking to PayPal

### Wellness Section
