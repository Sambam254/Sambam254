Resume Writing Tips for the Ontario Job Market

What Ontario employers actually want to see on your resume.

By Samantha Russell · May 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Home › Careers Hub › Resume Writing Tips for the Ontario Job Market

The Ontario job market is competitive, diverse, and rapidly evolving. Whether you are in Toronto, Oshawa, Hamilton, or anywhere in the province, understanding what local employers expect from a resume gives you a genuine advantage.

Know Your Market

Ontario's economy spans financial services (Toronto), manufacturing and automotive (Windsor, Oshawa), tech (Toronto, Waterloo), healthcare (throughout), and government (Ottawa, Toronto). Tailor your resume language to the specific sector and region where you are applying.

Canadian Resume Standards

  • No photo, no date of birth, no SIN
  • Contact info: name, phone, professional email, LinkedIn URL, city and province (full address not required)
  • Length: 1-2 pages for most professionals, up to 3 for senior executives
  • Language: clean, concise, active verbs (managed, led, developed, implemented)

Lead with a Strong Summary

Three to four lines at the top of your resume summarizing who you are, what you do, and what you bring. Tailor this to every application. This is the first thing recruiters read — make it count.

Quantify Your Accomplishments

Numbers make accomplishments concrete and memorable. "Reduced processing time by 30%" is far stronger than "improved efficiency." Revenue figures, team sizes, budget amounts, percentages, timelines — all of these make your achievements tangible.

Highlight Ontario-Specific Credentials

Professional designations common in Ontario (P.Eng., CPA, CHRP, PMP, RPN, etc.) belong prominently after your name and in your credentials section. Ontario employers recognize and value these certifications.

Format for Both Human and ATS Reading

Most Ontario employers with structured hiring use ATS. Use keyword-matched language from the job posting, clean formatting (no tables or text boxes), and standard section headings. Once past ATS, your resume needs to impress a human recruiter in under 10 seconds.
