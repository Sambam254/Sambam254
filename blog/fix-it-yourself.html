Fix It Yourself Queen: Empowering Self-Represented Litigants

November 28, 2025 · By Samantha Russell

Walking into a courtroom without a lawyer can feel like showing up to a chess match without knowing the rules. But here's the truth: thousands of Canadians successfully navigate the court system every year as self-represented litigants — and you can too.

At One Stop Therapy Shop, we believe that access to justice shouldn't be gatekept by the size of your bank account. That's why we created our legal document support services — to help you show up prepared, confident, and ready.

What Does "Self-Represented" Actually Mean?

A self-represented litigant (SRL) is someone who appears in court without a lawyer. You handle your own filings, write your own documents, and speak on your own behalf. It's challenging — but it's absolutely doable with the right preparation and support.

Where Most People Get Stuck

  • —Not knowing which forms to file or when
  • —Writing affidavits and factums that don't meet court standards
  • —Missing deadlines due to unfamiliarity with court timelines
  • —Feeling overwhelmed and not knowing where to start

How We Help

We don't give legal advice — but we do prepare, organize, and format your court documents to professional standards. We help you understand filing procedures, draft correspondence, and organize your arguments so you can walk in feeling like the queen you are.
