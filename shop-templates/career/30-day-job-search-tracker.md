## SAMANTHA'S JOB SEARCH TIPS

- Apply within 48 hours of a posting going live — early applicants get seen first
- Customize your resume for every application — at minimum, tailor the summary and skills
- Follow up by email 5-7 business days after applying if you have a contact name
- Quality beats quantity — 10 strong tailored applications outperform 100 generic ones
- Rejections are data, not failure — use them to refine your approach
- Set dedicated job search hours so it does not consume your whole day
- Track everything in this sheet so you can see patterns and improve week over week
