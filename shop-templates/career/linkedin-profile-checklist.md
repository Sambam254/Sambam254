# LinkedIn Profile Checklist — 25-Point Audit
**One Stop Therapy Shop — Career Templates**

---

> HOW TO USE THIS CHECKLIST
> Go through each section and check off every item. The more boxes you check, the more visible and compelling your profile will be to recruiters.
> LinkedIn's algorithm rewards complete, keyword-rich profiles with an "All-Star" rating — aim for that.

---

## SECTION 1: PROFILE PHOTO (3 points)

- [ ] 1. You have a profile photo (profiles with photos get 21x more views)
- [ ] 2. Your photo is professional — clear face, good lighting, appropriate background
- [ ] 3. Your photo is recent and looks like you today

---

## SECTION 2: BACKGROUND BANNER (2 points)

- [ ] 4. You have a custom banner image (not the default blue/grey)
- [ ] 5. Your banner reflects your industry, personal brand, or a professional message

---

## SECTION 3: HEADLINE (4 points)

- [ ] 6. Your headline is NOT just your job title — it tells people what you do and for whom
- [ ] 7. Your headline includes at least 2 relevant keywords from your target industry
- [ ] 8. Your headline is under 220 characters
- [ ] 9. Your headline is compelling enough to make someone want to click your profile

> Example of a weak headline: "Marketing Manager at XYZ Corp"
> Example of a strong headline: "Marketing Manager | Helping B2B SaaS Companies Grow Organic Traffic | Content Strategy & SEO"

---

## SECTION 4: ABOUT / SUMMARY SECTION (5 points)

- [ ] 10. Your About section is filled out (not blank)
- [ ] 11. It opens with a strong first sentence (the first 2 lines show before "see more")
- [ ] 12. It is written in first person and sounds like you — not a robotic job description
- [ ] 13. It includes 3-5 relevant keywords for your target role or industry
- [ ] 14. It ends with a clear call to action (e.g., "Open to new opportunities in X — feel free to connect")

---

## SECTION 5: EXPERIENCE SECTION (4 points)

- [ ] 15. All current and recent roles are listed with correct dates
- [ ] 16. Each role has at least 2-3 bullet points describing your responsibilities and achievements
- [ ] 17. You have used quantifiable results where possible (%, $, numbers, timeframes)
- [ ] 18. Your most recent role descriptions match or align with your resume

---

## SECTION 6: EDUCATION (1 point)

- [ ] 19. Your education is filled out with institution, degree, and years attended

---

## SECTION 7: SKILLS SECTION (3 points)

- [ ] 20. You have at least 10 skills listed
- [ ] 21. Your top 3 pinned skills are the most relevant to your target role
- [ ] 22. You have endorsements for your key skills (ask colleagues to endorse you)

---

## SECTION 8: RECOMMENDATIONS (1 point)

- [ ] 23. You have at least 1 written recommendation from a manager, colleague, or client

---

## SECTION 9: CONTACT & SETTINGS (2 points)

- [ ] 24. Your contact information is up to date (email, website, or portfolio link if applicable)
- [ ] 25. Your profile URL is customized (e.g., linkedin.com/in/samantharussell — not a string of numbers)

---

## BONUS: OPEN TO WORK SETTINGS

If you are actively job searching:
- [ ] Turn on "Open to Work" — you can choose to show it to all LinkedIn members or only recruiters
- [ ] Specify the job titles you are open to, your preferred work type, and location

---

## YOUR SCORE

Add up all checked boxes:

| Score | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| 22-25 | All-Star profile — maximum recruiter visibility |
| 18-21 | Strong profile — a few tweaks will make a big difference |
| 13-17 | Good start — focus on the missing sections |
| Below 13 | Your profile needs significant work — prioritize the basics first |

---

## QUICK TIPS FROM SAMANTHA

- Update your profile BEFORE you start applying — recruiters check LinkedIn after they see your resume
- Post or engage on LinkedIn 2-3 times per week to boost your visibility in the algorithm
- Connect with recruiters in your industry — even if you are not actively searching
- Use the "Featured" section to pin your resume, portfolio, or a key project
- Turn off activity broadcasts before making major edits so your network is not notified of every change
