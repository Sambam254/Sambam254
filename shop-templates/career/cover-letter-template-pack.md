## COVER LETTER TIPS FROM SAMANTHA

- Keep it to one page — under 400 words is ideal
- Never start with "My name is..." or "I am writing to apply..." — start with something stronger
- Mirror language from the job posting — if they say "client-facing," use that phrase
- Research the company before writing — one specific detail about them goes a long way
- Avoid restating your resume — use the letter to add context and personality
- Spell-check and read it aloud before sending — typos in cover letters are disqualifying
- Save as: YourName-CoverLetter-CompanyName.pdf
