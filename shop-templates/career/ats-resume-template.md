## ATS TIPS FROM SAMANTHA

- Use standard section headers: Professional Experience, Education, Skills — not creative alternatives
- Avoid graphics, logos, photos, columns, or fancy fonts — ATS systems cannot read them
- Do not put contact info in the header/footer — some ATS systems skip headers entirely
- Always customize your resume for each application — match the exact keywords in the job posting
- Save your file as: YourName-Resume-RoleName.pdf so recruiters can find it easily
- Keep dates consistent: Month Year format throughout (e.g., January 2022, not 01/22)
- Spell out acronyms at least once: Human Resources (HR), not just HR
