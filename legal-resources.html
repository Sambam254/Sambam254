Why This Hub Exists

The Legal System Is Hard Enough — Finding Resources Should Not Be

I am Samantha Russell — BA Criminology and Justice, legal document specialist, and founder of One Stop Therapy Shop in Oshawa, Ontario. I built this resource library because I have seen how overwhelming Ontario family court can be, especially when you are navigating it without a lawyer.

These guides are written in plain language, focused on Ontario procedures, and designed to help you walk into court prepared, organized, and confident. For hands-on document preparation, SafeGround provides professional support on a sliding-scale fee model — far more accessible than a lawyer.
